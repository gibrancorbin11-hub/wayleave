What the software does and does not do

This is worth being explicit about, because the subject matter is security and money:

  • Wayleave is a screening and pricing layer, not a guarantee. It verifies signatures that are presented and classifies traffic on the evidence available. It cannot promise that every automated request is caught, nor that a determined party cannot imitate a browser.
  • Settlement is not implemented. The 402 challenge and the payment-proof check work. No payment network is wired up. Wayleave does not move, hold, or take custody of funds, and it is not a payment processor, money transmitter, or escrow service.
  • Decisions it returns are advisory to your application. What you do with a lane classification — allow, deny, charge — is your configuration and your responsibility.

You are responsible for how you deploy it, what you charge, and for complying with the laws that apply to your own service.
