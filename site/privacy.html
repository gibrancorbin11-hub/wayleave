Privacy notice

Last updated 30 July 2026.

This is a one-page site with a waitlist form on it. There is no product account, no login, and no tracking. This notice describes everything that actually happens, in plain terms.

What is collected

Only what you type into the waitlist form:

  • Your email address. Required, because the entire purpose of the list is to email you once.
  • Your answer to the traffic question. Optional. A single choice from a dropdown. Leave it blank and nothing is recorded for it.

Nothing else is collected. There is no analytics script on this site, no advertising pixel, and no cookie is set. The light/dark toggle keeps your choice in memory for the current page view only — it is not stored and does not persist.

Where it goes

Form submissions are received and stored by Netlify, which hosts this site, using their Forms feature. They act as the processor; the data sits in that project's form submissions. Netlify's own handling is covered by their privacy policy.

Your address is not sold, rented, shared, or passed to any advertiser, data broker, or third-party mailing tool.

Server logs and fonts

Like any website, requests reach a server that records ordinary technical details such as IP address, browser user-agent, and the page requested. That is Netlify's standard hosting log, not something built for this site.

This page loads typefaces from two third parties — Fontshare and Google Fonts. Loading a font means your browser makes a request to those services, which means they can see your IP address. No font provider receives your email address or your form answer.

How the address is used

One email, if and when the hosted meter becomes a real product. That is the whole plan. No newsletter, no drip sequence, no "we thought you'd also like." If the meter never gets built, you may receive one email saying so, or nothing at all.

How long it is kept

Until the waitlist has served its purpose or you ask for removal, whichever comes first. This list exists to decide whether to build one specific thing; it is not a permanent marketing database.

Getting your data deleted

Email hello@wayleave.dev and ask. No form, no verification hoops, no "are you sure" sequence. You are entitled to ask what is held about you, to have it corrected, and to have it erased — and since the answer is "an email address and possibly one dropdown value," honoring that is straightforward.

Children

This is developer tooling. It is not directed at children and no part of it is designed to appeal to them.

Changes

If this notice changes materially while your address is on the list, the change will be reflected here with a new date at the top. Since the site collects so little, any meaningful change would mean the site started doing something new — and that is exactly the kind of thing worth telling you about.

Who is responsible

Wayleave is an independent open-source project maintained by Gibran Corbin. Questions about anything above: hello@wayleave.dev.
